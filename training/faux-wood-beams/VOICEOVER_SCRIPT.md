## 10 · Installation

**4:22.1** Installation, from the Ekena installation guide.

**4:25.8** Let the beams acclimate in the room for at least 24 hours. Then snap a chalk line down the center of each beam's path.

**4:33.8** Cut 2 by 4 blocking to the beam's inside width. Fasten the first block 2 feet from the wall, then one every 3 to 6 feet.

**4:42.6** Measure every run, cut the beam a little long, and dry fit it before any adhesive.

**4:49.1** Apply construction adhesive to the outside of the blocking and the top edges of the beam, press it in place, then fasten with finishing nails or screws.

**4:58.7** Clean off extra adhesive and caulk where the beam meets the wall. Cover any splice with a flexible beam strap.
