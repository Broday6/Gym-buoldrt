## 2. Names: Timberthane = Endurathane

The same Ekena beams appear as **Timberthane** (Ekena, Architectural Depot, newer Amazon
titles) and **Endurathane** (Lowe's, older Amazon, Home Depot titles), with the same
`BM…` SKUs; e.g. BMSD3C0060X040X168 is titled "Timberthane" on Ekena, and the BMSD3C
family is titled "Sandblasted Endurathane" on Amazon. (V)

## 4. Textures (Timberthane, 8) and SKU codes

Hand Hewn `HH` · Rough Sawn `RS` · Sandblasted `SD` · Riverwood `RW` · Knotty Pine `KP` ·
Pecky Cypress `PC` · Rough Cedar `RC` · Rustic Smooth `SM`. Eight textures (V). The codes
for HH, RS, SD, RW, KP, PC and SM appear in SKUs with matching titles (V). RC appears in
BMRC SKUs (e.g. BMRC2C0100X040X288RD); the title wasn't seen (I).

The short descriptions in the video (adze marks, saw marks, raised grain, water-worn,
knots, pockets, fibrous, cleanest) describe what each texture imitates. The swatches are
procedural illustrations, not photos.

## 6. Sizes and SKU anatomy

`BM SD 3C 0120 X 100 X 288 ZD` = beam · texture · profile · width 12.0" · height 10.0" ·
length 288" (24') · finish. Width/height are in tenths of an inch (0040 = 4", 0120 = 12");
length is in inches. Verified examples: 4×4, 6×4, 8×8, 10×12, 12×10, 12×12; lengths 96"
(8') to 288" (24'). (V)
Planks: `BMRWS1C0080X010X072ZH` = 8" W × 1.0" T × 72" L (code order W × T × L; titles say
W × L × T). (V)
Custom beam builder (Architectural Depot): up to 24" × 24" × 360" (30'). (S)
Some retailers list height first (Home Depot "6 in. H x 12 in. W" = BMSD3C0120X060X144CM;
Lowe's names like "4-in x 10-in x 240-in" for a 10"W × 4"H beam). (V)

## 8. Installation (Ekena install guide, via search excerpts)

1. Acclimate beams in the room at least 24 hours.
2. Mark center points and snap a chalk line along the beam path.
3. Cut wood blocking (2×4) to the beam's **inside** width; attach the first block 2' from the wall, then every 3–6'.
4. Measure each run; cut beams a little long; dry fit without adhesive.
5. Construction adhesive on the outside of the blocking and the top of the beam; press in place; fasten with finishing nails or screws.
6. Clean excess adhesive; caulk where beam meets wall.
Guide note: inside dimensions assume a ¼" tolerance per joint and a 1" beam wall thickness. (S)
