# Endura-Stone™ Columns — Research Fact Sheet

Source of truth for the training video in this folder. Every on-screen claim in
`index.html` traces to a line here. Confidence:
**V** = verified in two or more independent listings/documents ·
**S** = single source · **I** = inferred from a pattern in catalog data (flagged on screen).

> (architecturaldepot.com, ekenamillwork.com, pacificcolumns.com, homedepot.com,

## 3. Capitals and bases

Capitals (V, Pacific Columns round-tapered page, one product photo each): **Tuscan** (standard set),
**Roman Doric Ornamental**, **Roman Ionic**, **Scamozzi**, **Roman Corinthian**, **Temple of Winds**,
**Greek Angular Ionic**, **Greek Erechtheum** (also with necking), **Empire** (also with necking),
**Modern Composite**; plus **Craftsman** cap & base for square Pro Series.

Ornamental capitals (V, spec sheet + decorative-capital install PDFs): set on TOP of a tapered shaft after
it is cut at the neck ring, so they add to overall height; "not typically available for non-tapered round
shafts". There are separate **load-bearing** and **non-load-bearing** decorative-capital versions (the
non-load version uses a plug); **split capitals are not load bearing**.

Bases (V, Pacific Columns product pages): round tapered: **Tuscan**, **Attic** and **Roman Doric** sets;
round non-tapered: Tuscan or Attic; square: Tuscan, Attic, **Pedestal** base, **Craftsman** cap & base.

Capital/base material varies by SKU (V):
- ES1008RTPSATUTU: Tuscan **base (FRP)** with 13½" square plinth; Tuscan **capital (polyurethane)** with 12⅛" square abacus; shaft can be trimmed up to 34 11/16" from the bottom.
- Standard Tuscan cap/base on 8", 10", 12" round tapered: **TimeSaver™** fiber-reinforced (recycled) ABS.

|---|---|---|---|
| A | Unsplit / whole (round) | **Yes** — the only round plan with load ratings | V |
| B | ¼ cut out for **outside corner** | No | V |
| C | ¼ cut out for **pilaster** (¾ column on a wall) | No | V |
| D | Split in ½ for pilasters | No | V |
| Q | ¼ cut out for **inside corner** | No | V |
| R | All components split for reassembly (wrap around an existing post) | No — post carries the load | V |
| E | Unsplit / whole (square Pro Series) | Yes (whole shaft) | V |
| F | Square, split in half | No | V (Amazon B0H37N2XNH; Millwork.com plan-type F) |
| G | Square, all components cut out for an inside corner | No | V (Amazon B0H37KT5KL; Millwork.com ESP1214GNPSACRCR) |
| K | Square, cut out for a wall wrap | No | V |
| L | Square, cut out for outside flush wall wrap | No | S |

Rule from the installation guide: **split columns and pilasters are not load-bearing
and cannot be considered structural**. Never attach railings directly to them —
fasten to a structural member inside the column or the wall behind. (V)

## 5. Load ratings (Plan A / unsplit, published allowable loads)

| Bottom diameter | Concentric | Eccentric | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8" | 10,000 lb | 6,600 lb | V |
| 10" | 14,000 lb | 10,720 lb | V |
| 12" | 18,000 lb | 13,200 lb | V |
| 6" | 6,000 lb | 6,000 lb | V |
| 14"–24" | 20,000 lb | 14" 11,520; 16" 13,200; 18" 9,040; 20" 18,960; 24" 13,200 | V |

Concentric = load centred on the column axis; eccentric = off-centre load.
To reach rated capacity, load must be **fully and evenly distributed across the
entire top and bottom shaft surfaces**. (V)

Additional load facts (spec sheet / install guide, S–V): only the **shaft** carries load, not the
capital or base; shaft ends must be cut level; ratings are for **downward (axial) load only** (no lateral
load or uplift).

**Correction:** an earlier version said ratings "include a 4× safety factor". That figure is not in the
Endura-Stone spec sheet, warranty or load-test document, so it was removed.

**Load documentation (V):** Pacific Columns' "Endura-Stone column load tests" PDF is ICC-ES Legacy Report
22-26 (issued July 2004) for **Poly-Classic® FRP Columns**, report holder Cascade Ohio / CW Ohio. Its
Table 1 is identical to the Endura-Stone load table above (also: 8" non-tapered 10,000 / 8,240; 10"
non-tapered 14,000 / 11,520).

## 6. Reading a part number (I — pattern derived from catalog titles)

`ES 10 08 R T P S A TU TU` → line · diameter (in) · overall height (ft) · plan ·
taper (T = true entasis, N = non-tapered) · shaft (P = plain, F = fluted) ·
finish (S = smooth) · A = ready to be painted · base code · capital code.

Base-then-capital order holds on ES1609ATPSATURD (Tuscan base, Roman Doric capital),
ES2016RTPSAATRC (Attic base, Roman Corinthian), ES0608QTPSATUEM (Tuscan base, Empire).
Codes: TU Tuscan, AT Attic, TW Temple of Winds (e.g. ES2424ATPSAATTW, ESK1005DTPSATUTW), RD Roman Doric Ornamental, RI Roman Ionic,
RC Roman Corinthian, SC Scamozzi, GE Greek Erechtheum, EM Empire, CR Craftsman, PED Pedestal.
Prefixes: ESP = Pro Series (square); ESK appears on retail listings.
Exception: ESP0810KNPSATUPED is titled "Tuscan Capital & Pedestal Base", so
**always confirm the capital and base in the title**.

## 7. Installation essentials (V — Endura-Stone install & split-kit guides)

1. Measure from the load surface to the underside of the beam, plumb.
2. Trim the shaft **from the bottom**, ¼"–½" shorter than the measurement, with an abrasive blade. The trim allowance is set per SKU.
3. Slide the base on; apply construction adhesive to the top of the capital and the bottom of the base; align the square base to the load surface.
4. Flash the top: lead/copper/aluminum/galvanized flashing cut slightly larger than the cap, edges folded down.
5. Split columns: **Split Kit #72665** = 6 nylon locking straps, 6 × ⅛" spacers, 1 qt (1.85 lb) auto body filler. Two kits for columns over 12' tall or 14" diameter.
6. Pilasters: L-brackets top and bottom; pre-drill; don't over-tighten (the shaft can fracture).

## 9. Competitive set

**Turncraft is a related line, not a true competitor (V from primary docs):** the Endura-Stone spec sheet
tells installers to "contact Turncraft Customer Service" and says "Turncraft will normally ship the
Quick-Fit capitals"; Pacific Columns' Endura-Stone load-test PDF is the Poly-Classic evaluation report;
a Turncraft catalog is hosted on catalogs.pacificcolumns.com. The video calls it "a related line, not a
rival". The exact corporate relationship is not stated in these documents.

Some of these brands are also sold on ArchitecturalDepot.com (Turncraft, Fypon, AFCO
appear as ad groups in the 2026 Five-Line playbook).

| **Chadsworth PolyStone®** | Polyester resin, fiberglass, marble dust | Yes | Up to 20,000 lb (press materials; the earlier 25,000 figure could not be verified) | Lifetime | V (warranty) / S (load) |

Naming gotcha: AFCO sells **"Endura-Aluminum"** columns (EA… SKUs). These are
**not** Endura-Stone.

Selling caution: manufacturers test differently. Compare a specific SKU's
published rating to another specific SKU's, never "brand vs brand".

## Finish letters in the SKU (S)

9th character: **S** = smooth, **T** = textured. 10th: **A** = paint-grade, or a color code
(**D** = White, from ES1008ATPTDTUTU = 10" × 8' round tapered, textured White, Tuscan cap and
base). Other color letters were not found.
