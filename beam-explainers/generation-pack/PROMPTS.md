## S1: the "before" (same room, no beams)

**Why it's needed:** the video opens on a bare ceiling and wipes to the finished one on the beat.
The "after" is Ekena's own render, so the product is never generated. Only the before is an edit.

**Attach:** `refs/S1-after-room-BMSTKB-09.jpg`

**Prompt (an edit of the attached image):**
> Edit this photo. Remove only the dark wood ceiling beams: the ridge beam running up the middle and
> the four sloped rafters. Where they were, continue the plain white painted drywall ceiling, with the
> same soft daylight shading and a sharp crease where the two sloped ceiling planes meet. Keep the
> black chandelier exactly where it is, hanging on its rod from a small round canopy on the ceiling
> crease. Change nothing else: same camera, framing, lens, light, furniture, fireplace, windows, walls,
> colours and grain. Photoreal, ordinary daylight, not dramatic.

For the portrait version, ask for the same edit at 1024×1536, keeping the chandelier and fireplace centered.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] No beam, beam shadow or beam outline left anywhere on the ceiling
- [ ] Ceiling crease is crisp and in the same place
- [ ] Chandelier, rod and canopy intact
- [ ] Everything below the ceiling is identical to the reference (lay the two side by side)
- [ ] No new objects, no lighting change

**Why it's needed:** the weight objection. The video says: "A twelve-foot beam weighs about eighteen
pounds. One person can lift it, and install it." On screen it says **18 lb** and **5½ × 5½ in × 12 ft**.
Sources: the product record for BMSTS3C00600X0600X144KB (weight 18.00 lb) and Ekena's key feature
"Simple one-person installation", plus the description "one person can comfortably lift and install".

**Attach:**
- `refs/S2-ref-beam-full-BMSTKB.jpg`
- `refs/S2-ref-hollow-end-BMSTKB-05.jpg`
- `refs/S2-ref-beam-angle-BMSTKB-01.jpg`

**Prompt:**
> Photoreal image. One adult (late 30s, casual work clothes, no logos) stands in a bright, freshly
> painted living room with a plain white vaulted ceiling and wood floors, holding ONE faux wood beam
> horizontally at chest height with both hands, easily and without strain. The beam is exactly the
> product in the attached photos: Heritage Timber Salvaged Timber texture in Kona Brown, a 3-sided U-beam,
> 5½ inches square in section and 12 feet long, so it is about twice the person's height. The open,
> hollow U-shaped end with its cream-colored inner walls faces the camera, as in the hollow-end
> reference. Match the texture and finish to the references exactly. Do not invent knots, colours or
> grain. A step ladder and a short wood mounting block lie on the floor nearby. Ordinary daylight from
> the windows, eye-level camera, the whole beam in frame. No text, no logo.

For the portrait version, ask for 1024×1536. The person can stand slightly to one side so the full
beam still fits diagonally.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Exactly one person, relaxed grip, no strain
- [ ] The beam is the right length (about 2× the person's height), 3-sided, hollow end visible with cream inside
- [ ] Texture and colour match Salvaged Timber, Kona Brown (compare with the swatch)
- [ ] Hands, fingers and face look natural
- [ ] No text or logos anywhere
- [ ] Daylight, nothing theatrical
