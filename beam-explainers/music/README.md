# Music

`ondrosik-peaceful.flac`: "Peaceful" by Ondrosik (Ondrej Rosik), from his Free Music Catalog on the
Internet Archive: https://archive.org/details/Ondrosik-Free-music-catalog
(file: https://archive.org/download/Ondrosik-Free-music-catalog/Peaceful.flac).

Licence: the item is marked CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), and the composer's own licence file
(`ondrosik-license.html`, saved here as downloaded) allows use in videos and films, editing, syncing to
picture, monetised distribution with no royalties, and says attribution is welcome but not required.

`peaceful-beats.json`: beat times (librosa), used by `tools/timeline.py --beats` to put every scene
change on a beat. The track opens up at 55.8 s; the end card is anchored there.
