# House rules: Ekena beam explainer videos

Rules Brody has set. They override general practice. One per line, with where each came from.

1. Fully coded videos: no stock footage, no AI-generated video, no editing software. ffmpeg only encodes. (Brody, 8 Oct 2026)
2. Show real Ekena product images (use the best available: Ekena's own room renders and studio shots ahead of customer photos; Brody, 9 Oct 2026: "use better imagery") whenever the product, a finish or a texture is on screen. Coded models are only for things that aren't the beams: ceilings, blocks, calendars, people for scale, UI. (Brody, 8 Oct 2026)
3. Nothing is published until it has been through the Review Studio, approved there, and cleared by publish_gate.py. (Brody, Oct 2026)
4. Brand: Ekena green, Poppins headlines and Figtree body text, and the real Ekena logo. (Video pipeline description, Oct 2026)
5. Narration is Kokoro, said phonetically where needed: Ekena is "EE-ken-uh". A local speech-recognition check must hear every key term. (Video pipeline description, Oct 2026)
6. Music: a real, copyright-free recording (public domain / CC0, or a licence that allows commercial video use with no royalties), kept quiet under the narration and with the edit timed to its beats. The licence is saved next to the track and named in the README. The music is ducked under the narration. 60 fps. Both 1920×1080 and 1080×1920, H.264. (Brody, 9 Oct 2026: "find copy right free music that you can add to the background and time the video to. have the music be quite"; replaces the earlier synthesized-score rule.)
