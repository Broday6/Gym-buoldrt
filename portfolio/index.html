        How I work
        
      
        Four rules that show up in everything above.
        
    
        
    
        
      
        The real product wins.
        Generated scenes can change the room, never the product. Finish, count and scale come from real photos.
        
      
        Measure before claiming.
        Each claim about Compass has a test, a benchmark or an audit behind it, and the numbers on this page come from those runs.
        
      
        People make the call.
        Audits recommend. Spend moves, launches and go-live decisions wait for a person to say yes.
        
      
        Stop and say why.
        When a step can't be done properly, the system names what's blocked and what would fix it instead of shipping something lesser.
